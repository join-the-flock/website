# About Flock

<p class="page-lead">Flock is Flutter++. A fork that stays up to date with Flutter, adds new features, and fixes bugs on top.</p>

## Why Flock exists

A bug or missing feature in Flutter can stand between an app and its next release. Flock gives developers another way forward: a shared fork where the community can contribute the fixes and features they need.

Our mission is to help teams keep shipping with Flutter. You can use Flock while you need its changes, then switch back to Flutter when you no longer do.

## Built on Flutter

Flock rebases daily on top of Flutter’s master, beta, and stable branches. It builds on Flutter’s work while making room for additional community contributions.

[Read our mission](/flock/mission) or [get started with Flock](/flock/get-started).
