Generated with the built-in image-generation tool using the supplied nest artwork as the subject reference and the supplied Flock logo as the style reference. Transparent master saved at `design/logos/nest/nest-logo-v1.png`.

## Prompt

Use case: logo-brand.
Create ONE simplified Nest logo, using the first reference for the subject and the second reference for the visual language.

Subject: the nest of eggs in reference 1, radically simplified into a crisp compact logo that is legible when displayed only 32–36 pixels tall beside a website name. Keep a bold golden-orange nest bowl and the recognizable egg grouping: hot pink central Fleek egg with a purple swept crest, golden egg on the left, deep plum egg on the right, and a simple ivory egg peeking from behind. Keep the central pink egg visually dominant. Small confident eyes and tiny beaks may be represented with only a few large shapes; no detailed feathers, intricate faces, or speckles.

Art direction from the Flock logo in reference 2: clean sharp illustrative logo shapes, strong expressive silhouettes, hot pink and electric violet accents, deep ink-purple negative spaces, a small cyan eye accent, only a few broad flat color facets or very restrained gradients. Match its graphic simplicity rather than the glossy 3D painting of reference 1. Make the nest from just TWO OR THREE thick, interlocking curved golden strokes, not a mass of detailed twigs. Remove leaves, fine highlights, tiny floating droplets and ornamental detail.

Composition: compact near-square emblem, tighten the eggs together and raise the bowl so the mark has enough height and visual weight to sit next to the Flock logo. Clean readable silhouette, balanced proportions, modest transparent padding. One logo only, centered on a SQUARE CANVAS.

Background: genuine transparent alpha. No colored or white rectangle, no checkerboard rendered into the art, no ground or cast shadow, no atmospheric glow, no circle or surrounding badge, NO words or letters, no tagline, no comparison sheet.
